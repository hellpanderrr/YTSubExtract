Privacy Policy for YTSubExtract
YTSubExtract is a Free and Open Source (FOSS) browser extension. We believe in total transparency and user privacy.
🛡️ Data Collection & Transmission
No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
No Tracking: There are no analytics, telemetry, or tracking scripts bundled with this extension.
Local Processing: All subtitle extraction and format conversions (SRT, VTT, TXT) happen locally within your browser.
Direct Requests: When translating or fetching captions, the extension communicates directly with YouTube™/Google APIs. No third-party servers are involved.
💾 Storage
We use the chrome.storage.local API solely to remember your preferred UI settings (e.g., target translation language and checkbox states). This data never leaves your device.
🔓 Third-Party Services
This extension interacts with YouTube™ and Google servers to fetch subtitle data. These interactions are subject to Google's own Privacy Policy.
📝 Open Source
Since this is an open-source project, you can audit the source code at any time on our GitHub repository.
